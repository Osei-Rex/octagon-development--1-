A wholly indigenous EPC company, built for national‑scale delivery.

Octagon Development Limited was established to close the gap between Ghana's infrastructure ambitions and the local capacity to deliver them — pairing Ghanaian engineering talent with the procurement discipline and construction standards that major projects demand.

From energy infrastructure to industrial and commercial development, we partner with government bodies, private developers and international capital to take projects from feasibility through to commissioning.

01 / ENGINEERING

Design & Feasibility

Octagon Development Limited possesses the technical expertise, engineering capability, and project management systems required to execute the rehabilitation and restoration of underground fire water pipeline infrastructure within operational industrial facilities. Our multidisciplinary team is experienced in delivering complex projects while maintaining strict compliance with client HSE requirements and minimizing disruption to plant operations.

02 / PROCUREMENT

Sourcing & Supply

Octagon Development Limited is a duly registered indigenous company with the Ministry of Roads and Highways as an A1 B1 Road Construction Contractor. The company is also registered with the Ministry of Works and Housing under the D1 category, authorizing it to undertake general building and civil works. In addition, Octagon Development is listed in the official database of the Public Procurement Authority A1.
